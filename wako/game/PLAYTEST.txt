Double-click Play_Island_Workshop.cmd to start at the real Island workshop.
This session has active enemies, normal damage and finite physical parts.
Its saves use the launcher's existing separate manual-play data folder.
F5 saves a quicksave; F9 restores it, including after relaunching.

Try this route:
1. Open Build with Ctrl+B. On Start, choose the two-piece powered lamp
   or the starter rover. Read its required stock and crew status.
2. Close Build and watch the Wako fetch actual stock on their heads,
   fit tools with their feet, and assemble the selected plan.
3. Reopen Build to select an unfinished or finished assembly. Try the
   snapped customisation preview and its rotate/invalid-placement hints.
4. Enter an installed helm through its displayed prompt. Drive, change
   camera view and brake. The contextual shortcut panel shows current
   bindings; H opens it. Controls can be rebound in Settings.
5. Hold Tab for the tool wheel. Equip a gun before opening Build > Guns.
   Lend it to an idle nearby Wako, close Build to watch it defend with a
   clear shot within 25 m, then take the same gun back before asking it
   to Follow. Installed gun sockets use real control points.
6. Open the map with M. Explore machine territories, defeat guards and
   use the captive's rescue prompt. Aim at a recruited Wako and use
   Ctrl+F for Follow/Stay; Ctrl+H returns it to useful work.
7. Study a defeated machine, then recover its physical stock. Bring it
   back to the workshop and select useful work. Save and load along the
   way to check that recruited helpers, plans and finite parts persist.
8. In Build, select a machine and choose Dismantle selected. Review its
   installed parts before confirming recovery; watch the crew's status.

Coastal camps now show earned discovery and rescue guidance on approach.
For manual salvage, equip Grabber from Tab and hold left click on a part.
Use the displayed Salvage / inspect action to detach donor stock, then grab
the loose body again. Keep holding it and walk clear of the wreck before
dropping it, so the Wako has room to launch and pick it up on its head.
Open Build and Resume work if the crew is paused. The Wako carries this
same battery to the depot; its condition and charge survive F5/F9 and
the final stored save. The game explains that held parts must be dropped.
Approach idle Wako to see their eye-led greeting; useful work interrupts it.
The gallery includes bare and back-mallet greetings and actual fitting sound.
Delivery from distant camps, full dismantling and the complete
player/Wako/mounted-gun sequence are still being finished.
Current media labels distinguish Island gameplay from focused checks.

Use F12 at the moment a problem occurs. It captures the game state and
screenshots in .claude/feedback/inbox so the problem can be reproduced.

Open FeatureGallery/OpenGallery.cmd (or the Desktop Gameplay Gallery
shortcut) for named pictures and autoplay videos, newest first. Choose
Pictures or Videos to filter. Its report button saves a gallery report
to the same inbox; an in-game F12 report includes more live-game detail.
