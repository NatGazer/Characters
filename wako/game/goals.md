# Wako and contraption goals

The final gameplay direction takes precedence: Wako helpers choose useful jobs automatically. Players select blueprints and control locomotion; Wako occupy utility and weapon stations, never drive. Reuse the existing Wako asset as the starting point, but improve its bones, weights, animations and geometry where needed. Wako have no arms: they manipulate tools and gun controls with their feet, using balanced acrobatics, and transport cargo on their heads. Check a goal only after its implementation has been run and verified. No marketing or story work.
